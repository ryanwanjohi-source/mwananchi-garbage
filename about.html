Mwananchi Garbage is a waste collection company started by Nairobi residents who were tired of irregular collections — trucks that came some weeks and not others, and waste piling up at estate gates. "Mwananchi" means the ordinary citizen, and that is who we work for: households and small businesses that need their waste gone, on a day they can plan around.

We run fixed routes on fixed days. Every customer knows their collection day, every crew follows the same route sheet, and every load is taken to a licensed disposal site or sorted for recycling. If a truck breaks down, a second truck covers the route the same day — the collection still happens.

What we stand for

  • On time. Your collection day does not change from week to week.
  • Honest pricing. One monthly price, agreed before we start. No charges at the gate.
  • Clean finish. The crew sweeps the bin area before they leave.
  • Less to landfill. Paper, plastic, glass and metal are sorted and sent for recycling.

Collection notices

  • Put your bin out by 7 a.m. on your collection day — the truck does not come back for late bins.
  • Public holidays: collections move to the next day.
