Missed collection?

If your bin was out by 7 a.m. and we did not come, call the office the same day. A cover truck is sent the following morning, free.

Collection notices

  • Put your bin out by 7 a.m. on your collection day — the truck does not come back for late bins.
  • Public holidays: collections move to the next day.
