Mwananchi Garbage collects household and business waste across Nairobi, on fixed days, for a fixed monthly price. No waiting for a truck that may not come, and no burning or dumping — everything we collect goes to licensed disposal and recycling sites.

What we collect

  • Household collection
  • Business collection
  • Recycling
  • Skip hire

How it works

  1. Choose your plan on the pricing page and book your first collection.
  2. We deliver your bin and tell you your collection day.
  3. Put the bin out by 7 a.m. on your day — we empty it and take everything away.

Collection notices

  • Put your bin out by 7 a.m. on your collection day — the truck does not come back for late bins.
  • Public holidays: collections move to the next day.
  • Need an extra pickup? Book one here.
